Mechanical Engineer · CAD, Fabrication & Data Analysis

Hi, I'm Arman.

I'm a mechanical engineer who likes taking a product from an early concept sketch through CAD, prototyping, and manufacturing considerations. My work spans SolidWorks and Rhino/Grasshopper modeling, mold design and casting-based fabrication, and hands-on prototype testing and material selection. Alongside the hardware side, I write code (Python, MATLAB) for data processing and analysis — turning experimental and sensor data into usable results.

This site collects my research, unpublished builds, course projects, and hobby work — CAD files, code, renders, and simulation data, all in one place.
