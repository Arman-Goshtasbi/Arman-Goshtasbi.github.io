About me

I'm currently a PhD candidate in Biorobotics at the University of Southern Denmark, where I develop soft tactile sensors and fabrication methods for soft robotics. Before that, I earned an M.S. in Mechanical Engineering (Robotics) from the University of Twente and a B.Sc. in Mechanical Engineering from the University of Tehran. I've had the opportunity to work in soft robotics and surgical robotics laboratories, as well as spend time as a visiting researcher at ETH Zurich.
