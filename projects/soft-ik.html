Published Research — TU/e Internship

Soft robot inverse kinematics

A geometric inverse kinematics model for multi-segment, extensible soft pneumatic actuators, developed during a mechanical engineering internship at TU/e.

← All projects

Overview

Soft pneumatic actuators are hard to model due to their nonlinear, continuous dynamics and hyper-elastic material behavior. This project approximates each segment of a multi-segment extensible soft robot as a chain of rigid links connected by rotary and prismatic joints, then solves the inverse kinematics numerically to track a desired end-effector trajectory — using the robot's redundancy for secondary objectives such as tip-angle control. The method was validated on a 3-segment, 3-bellow physical prototype (shown in the Media tab) and published in Sensors.

M. Keyvanara, A. Goshtasbi, I. A. Kuling, "A Geometric Approach towards Inverse Kinematics of Soft Extensible Pneumatic Actuators Intended for Trajectory Tracking," Sensors, vol. 23, no. 15, p. 6882, 2023. doi.org/10.3390/s23156882 →
