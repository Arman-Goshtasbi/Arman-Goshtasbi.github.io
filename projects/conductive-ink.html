Printable conductive silicone

A silicone-based conductive ink, mixable into standard soft robotics materials and printable by direct ink writing with no modifiers required.

← All projects

Overview

This ink mixes directly into Ecoflex and Dragon Skin — two of the most widely used silicones in soft robotics — to make them electrically conductive, reaching over 10 S/m while still stretching up to 300%. It's rheologically suited for direct ink writing (DIW) straight out of the mix, with no rheology modifiers needed, and has been printed on three platforms: a planar 3-axis printer, a 4-axis setup printing onto a rotating cylindrical rod, and a 5-axis setup printing onto a rotating spherical surface. This is active, ongoing work — expect updates here as the project develops over the next few months.
