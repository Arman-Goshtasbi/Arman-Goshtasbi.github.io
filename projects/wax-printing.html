Lost-cast wax 3D printing

Fabricating intricate, miniature soft robots in a single shot of molding, using 3D-printed wax molds instead of machined or multi-part rigid molds.

← All projects

Overview

Lost-cast wax molding uses a 3D-printed wax (or wax-like) core to define intricate, often multi-channel internal geometries that would be difficult or impossible to demold conventionally. The wax mold is cast around, then melted or dissolved out, leaving a single-shot soft actuator or sensor with fine internal detail and no assembly step. This is an active, ongoing project — designs cover both single-material actuators (bending, twisting, contracting, origami-based, and a fluid tactile sensor) and multi-material actuators combining stiff and soft regions in one mold.
