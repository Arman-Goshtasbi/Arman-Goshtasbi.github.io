Weld n'Cut

An open-source, CNC-controlled platform that combines ultrasonic welding and oscillating-knife cutting to automate the fabrication of inflatable fabric actuators.

← All projects

Overview

Textile-based inflatable soft actuators are normally fabricated in separate steps — heat-sealing fabric layers with a press, then manually cutting away non-stick masking layers that defined the internal chambers — a slow, error-prone process. Weld n'Cut combines both steps into a single automated platform built on a CNC gantry (CNC-STEP High-Z S-720): a 500 W ultrasonic spot welder, converted from a manual tool into a programmable one, welds the actuator's internal pattern, then an oscillating tangential knife cuts its boundary — both coordinated over the same G-code toolpath, with no manual masking step in between. The platform was used to fabricate four actuator classes: linear (contraction) PneuNets, unidirectional and antagonistic bending actuators, twisting actuators (via inclined weld lines), and kirigami actuators with staggered cut patterns for large, tunable contraction.

This work was supported by the Independent Research Fund Denmark (Sapere Aude grant 1051-00075B) and the Villum Young Investigator grant 37499.

A. Goshtasbi, B. Seyidoğlu, S. P. Murali Babu, A. Parvaresh, C. D. Do, A. Rafsanjani, "Weld n'Cut: Automated Fabrication of Inflatable Fabric Actuators," IEEE 8th International Conference on Soft Robotics (RoboSoft), 2025. arxiv.org/abs/2502.06361 →
