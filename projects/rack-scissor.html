Bachelor Project — Mechanical Design

Rack-driven scissor mechanism

A motor-driven scissor lift actuated by a rack-and-pinion system rather than a lead screw or linear actuator.

← All projects

Overview

A motor drives a central shaft carrying a pinion gear at each end. Each pinion meshes with a rack mounted on a sliding bracket, so as the shaft rotates, both brackets are driven along the base symmetrically. This motion drives the scissor linkage, raising the lift top. Using a rack-and-pinion drive on both sides keeps the two sides of the scissor mechanism synchronized without needing a separate control system. (Description drafted from the CAD model — let me know if any part of the mechanism works differently and I'll correct it.)
