Aluminum Additive Manufacturing Cooling System

A liquid-cooled tool housing designed to keep the material feed cool during a solid-state additive manufacturing process, preventing clogging while cladding high-strength aluminum.

← All projects

Overview

This project was part of a research assistantship during my bachelor's degree, done in collaboration between the University of Tehran and the University of Twente. The goal was to cool the material feed during a solid-state additive manufacturing process used to clad 6060 aluminum alloy, preventing the feed from clogging as it passes through the tool. Water enters through the inlet, passes through grooved channels that increase contact area for better heat transfer, and exits through the outlet on the other side. The inner part is shrink-fit into the housing, and two inserts seal the assembly so the coolant is contained within the channels rather than leaking out along the feed path, secured with side screw inserts.
